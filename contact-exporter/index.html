How it works

On your new phone in three steps

Once it's installed, it takes less than a minute.

01

Open WhatsApp Web

Sign in at web.whatsapp.com with the QR code and wait for your chats to load.

02

Click the extension icon

Your contacts are listed. Search, filter, and untick anyone you don't want.

03

Open the file on your phone

Send yourself the VCF file and open it. All contacts are added to your address book at once.

Privacy

Your address book stays with you

A contact list is sensitive data. The extension only reads it, writes it to a file and forgets it.

Permissions it asks for 2

  • activeTabAccess only to the tab you click the icon on, only at that moment. It never runs in the background.
  • scriptingTo run the small function that reads the contact list in that tab.

What it never touches none

  • Messages and mediaIt doesn't read chats, send messages, or change anything in WhatsApp.
  • ServersThere isn't a single line of code that makes a network request. Your contacts go nowhere.
  • Other sites and historyNo access to your browsing history or any other tab.
Check it yourself: all of the logic is in one file, extension/lib.js
FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How do I import the VCF file on an iPhone?
  1. Email it to yourself or send it with AirDrop.
  2. Tap the file on your iPhone and choose "Add All Contacts".
And on Android?

Send the file to your phone and tap it; the Contacts app offers to import it. If it doesn't, use Contacts → Settings → Import → .vcf file.

Can I import into Google Contacts?

Yes. The easiest way is to upload the VCF file at contacts.google.com → Import. If you use the CSV instead, turn off the "I'll open the CSV in Excel" option.

Which phone number formats are supported?

For CSV you can choose +905321234567, +90 532 123 45 67, the local format of more than 240 countries (for example (202) 555-0123 in the US or 0151 23456789 in Germany), 00905321234567, or digits only. VCF always uses the international format, so it works on every phone.

Why are some contacts missing?

The extension exports the contacts WhatsApp Web knows about: people who use WhatsApp and are saved in your address book. People without WhatsApp, and details such as email addresses or second numbers, aren't in WhatsApp, so they can't be exported. You can also include numbers you never saved with the "Also include numbers not saved in your contacts" option.

Is it safe for my WhatsApp account?

The extension sends nothing to WhatsApp, sends no messages and automates nothing. It only reads the contact list that WhatsApp Web already stores in your browser.

Which languages does it support?

English and Turkish. It opens in Turkish if your Chrome is set to Turkish and in English otherwise. Country names and CSV column headers follow the same language.

I get a "No contact data found" error.

Reload the page first and wait until your chats have fully loaded. If it keeps happening, WhatsApp Web may have changed its internals. Send me the "Technical details" text from the error screen on GitHub or by email and I'll fix it.

Does it cost anything?

No. It's free, ad-free and open source under the MIT license.

Ready to get your contacts back?

Install it, open WhatsApp Web, click. Everything else is one file away.
